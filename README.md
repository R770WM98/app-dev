# Favorite Movie : **The Matrix (1999)**
------------------------------

**Release date:** March 24, 1999 (Mann Village Theater)

### Credits

*   **Directed by:** The Wachowskis
    
*   **Written by:** The Wachowskis
    
*   **Produced by:** Joel Silver
    
*   **Cinematography by:** Bill Pope
    
*   **Edited by:** Zach Staenberg
    
*   **Music by:** Don Davis
    

### Production companies

1.  **Village Roadshow Pictures**
    
2.  **Groucho II Film Partnership**
    
3.  **Silver Pictures**
    

### Principal cast

1.  **Keanu Reeves** — Neo
    
2.  **Laurence Fishburne** — Morpheus
    
3.  **Carrie-Anne Moss** — Trinity
    
4.  **Hugo Weaving** — Agent Smith
    
5.  **Joe Pantoliano** — Cypher
    

Summary
-------

The Matrix follows Thomas A. Anderson (Neo), a computer programmer who discovers that the world he knows is a simulated reality created by sentient machines. Recruited by Morpheus and guided by Trinity, Neo learns to bend the rules of the simulation and faces a choice that will determine the fate of humanity.

Key Themes
----------

*   Reality vs simulation
    
*   Choice and free will
    
*   Identity and transformation
    
*   Technology and control
    
*   Hero's journey and messianic symbolism
    

Notable Sequences
-----------------

*   Bullet-dodging training sequence
    
*   Lobby shootout and slow-motion choreography
    
*   Neo learning to fight in the dojo
    
*   The rooftop jump and helicopter extraction
    
*   Final showdown with Agent Smith
    

Technical Notes and Style
-------------------------

*   **Visual style:** High-contrast green tint used for in-Matrix scenes; cool desaturated tones for the real world.
    
*   **Effects:** Pioneering use of "bullet time" and wire-fu choreography combined with practical stunts.
    
*   **Sound design:** Minimalist, with electronic score that underscores tension and scale.
    

Soundtrack / Score Highlights
-----------------------------

*   **Main themes:** Don Davis orchestral cues; licensed tracks used in trailers and marketing.
    
*   **Notable cues:** Opening credits theme; training montage underscore; climax string expansion.
    

Box Office & Reception (summary)
--------------------------------

*   **Initial impact:** Strong box office, influential cult and mainstream success.
    
*   **Legacy:** Spawned sequels, large fandom, academic discussion, and major influence on pop culture, fashion, and filmmaking techniques.
    

Filming & Locations
-------------------

*   **Primary filming:** Sydney, Australia
    
*   **Notable sets:** Dojo, Nebuchadnezzar interiors, city exteriors (composited and practical locations)
    

Trivia
------

*   The green tint for Matrix scenes was achieved by shifting the color timing on digital intermediates.
    
*   Stunt coordinators trained actors in wire-work months before principal photography.
    
*   The film popularized a renewed interest in cyberpunk aesthetics in mainstream cinema.
